    Questions

    Do I have to give up my current citizenship?

    No. Poland allows dual citizenship, and you are confirming a citizenship you already hold by descent.

    Do I need to travel to Poland?

    No. Your proxy in Poland files the application. You sign a power of attorney and send original or certified documents by courier.

    Are you a law firm?

    No. We are a document and filing service. Polish law allows any adult to act as your proxy in this administrative procedure. If you want a legal opinion or court representation, you can hire an independent Polish attorney directly; we take no fees from attorneys.

    What if my ancestor's documents are missing?

    Most records can be found in Polish civil and church archives. Our capped archive search covers the request, the fees and the follow-up.

    How long does it take?

    We prepare and file within about 4 to 8 weeks of receiving your documents. The Mazowiecki Voivode Office then takes around 18 to 24 months to decide.
